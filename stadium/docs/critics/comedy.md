# Rubric: comedy, animation and comic timing

The game is adult, dark, British satire (think the tone of a tabloid, a pub, a terrace). You are not asked
whether it is funny: real players decide that. You are asked whether the jokes are **set up to land**.

1. **Is the joke visible?** The punchline happens on screen, at a size and moment the player will see; text
   or interface does not cover it.
2. **Set-up and pay-off.** Was the joke seeded earlier (a line, an object, a routine) so the payoff reads?
3. **Timing.** Beats are neither rushed nor laboured; control is taken away only when it must be (a payoff
   cut should be no more than about 15 seconds); a line stays up long enough to read and no longer.
4. **Performance.** Characters act the joke (pose, motion, reaction) rather than a caption explaining it.
   Note where the joke depends on text because nobody performs it.
5. **Writing.** Lines are short, specific, British, in character; no exposition; no tutorial voice.
6. **Escalation.** Small mischief grows into something absurd, and each step is a little bigger.
7. **Repetition.** The same line, bark or gag repeating until it is dead.
8. **Sound.** (From the event log: what spoke, what played.) Is anything carried by sound that should be?

Point to frames and seconds for every finding.
