# Playtest: observer's sheet (first-time players on a phone)

**Who:** five people who have never seen the game. Not gamers only. Their own phones, portrait.
**What:** about 15 minutes each, one at a time, alone with you. Do not help, explain or demonstrate.
**Link:** the live game with `?playtest` added: https://krelworld.github.io/Glory-Quest/?playtest
(3D: add `&3d=1`.) The recorder times everything and gives you a laugh button; at the end, open the menu,
"Playtest report", copy it, and send it with your notes.

## Say only this
"This is a game about a football fan on matchday. Play it however you like; think out loud if you can. I
can't help you, but I'm interested in everything you think."

## While they play, note (with the clock time)
- The first moment they do something on purpose, and what.
- Every time they laugh, smile or swear at the screen, and what caused it (press the laugh button too).
- Every time they say they don't know what to do, what something is, or why something happened.
- Every mistap (pressed the wrong thing, missed a button).
- Every time they mention clutter, text, or not being able to see something.
- If they get stuck for more than a minute.
- If they stop wanting to play.

## Afterwards, ask (and write down their own words)
1. "What happened, as you understood it?"
2. "Did anything happen because of something you did? What, and how did you know?"
3. "Was there anything you wanted to do but couldn't work out how?"
4. "What was the funniest thing?"
5. "Would you play again? What would you try?"

## What we score (the experiment's criteria)
First intentional reaction within 60 s · can explain why a payoff happened · found at least one way to
set something up without help · laughs at something they caused · no clutter complaints · mistaps under
20% · wants to try another scheme · never stuck for over a minute.
