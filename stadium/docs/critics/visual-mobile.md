# Rubric: visual quality and mobile usability

You are judging what a player sees on a portrait iPhone (390 × 844 points) and, where given, landscape.
Look at the contact sheet first, then open the frames you need at full size.

Standing complaints from the game's creator (check every one; these have recurred):
1. **Visual noise and text.** "A total mess of visual noise with text." More than two pieces of text on
   screen at once (speech bubbles, boards, hint lines, strips, cards) is a problem; text covering the action
   or each other is a blocker. `metrics.md` gives the measured text load; check it against what you see.
2. **Can't tell what anything does.** Things you can use or ride should say what they do, where they go and
   how long; the main button's line should make sense.
3. **Can't pick out who matters.** The person or thing a player needs to notice should stand out from the
   crowd at a glance (colour, position, motion), without a label.
4. **Static, lifeless people.** People standing in lines or grids, or frozen, look ridiculous.
5. **Looks cheap.** Large plain surfaces, flat cards standing in a 3D world, mismatched pixel density,
   stretched or blurry sprites, things drawn through walls.

Also judge:
6. **Composition at phone size.** Is Grozz easy to find? Is the interesting thing on screen? Are the
   thumb-zone controls (lower left stick, lower right action) clear of what matters?
7. **Legibility.** Text readable at phone size; contrast; nothing important under the score or buttons.
8. **Coherence.** One art direction throughout (palette, light, pixel density, perspective).
9. **Feedback.** When something happens, can you see it happen, or is it off screen or hidden?

Do not judge whether the game is funny or fun here; another critic does that.
