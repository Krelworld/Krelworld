# KRELWORLD on a phone: the ten-minute check

This is for you (or a mate) to try the phone version on a real iPhone and a real Android phone once it is on the
live link. Computers can pretend to be phones, but they cannot tell us everything: how much memory a phone really
has, what the sound does, where your thumbs land, how hot it gets. Ten minutes per phone is plenty.

You need:
- an iPhone and an Android phone (any you have; older and cheaper ones are the most useful),
- the game's usual link (the one you send in the group chat),
- a way to take screenshots (iPhone: side button + volume up; Android: power + volume down).

Before you start, write down for each phone: the model (e.g. "iPhone 12", "Samsung A14") and the system version
(iPhone: Settings > General > About > iOS Version; Android: Settings > About phone > Android version).

## The read-out (`?mobilediag`)

Put `?mobilediag` on the end of the link, like this: `…/Krelworld/?mobilediag` (if the link already has a `?` in it,
use `&mobilediag` instead). A small black box of green writing appears in a corner of the game. Tap it to move it to
another corner. It shows:

| Line | What it means | What we hope to see |
|---|---|---|
| `fps` | how smoothly the game runs (frames a second) | 50 to 60 at home and in town; tell us the lowest you see |
| `textures` | how much picture memory the game is using | just write it down (it should say about 900 MB) |
| `sound` | how much sound is loaded, and whether sound is `running` | `running` after your first tap |
| `window` / `visual` / `dpr` | the size of the screen the game gets | just write them down |
| `canvas … scale` | how big the game picture is drawn | just write it down |
| `safe area L R T B` | how far the game keeps away from the notch and the bar at the bottom | on a home-screen iPhone with a notch, L and R should be about 47 to 59 and B about 21 |
| `touch mode` | which controls are showing | `walk` while walking about |
| `phone mode ON by device` | the game knows it is on a phone, and why | it must say ON. If it says off, tell us straight away |
| `last error` | the last thing that went wrong | `none`. If it says anything else, screenshot it |

Take a screenshot of the box at the start, at home, and in town (Krelford).

## iPhone (about 10 minutes)

1. **Open the link in Safari** with `?mobilediag` on the end. Hold the phone **sideways**. Tap the screen to enter.
2. **The "Add KRELWORLD to your Home Screen" box** should pop up by itself over the title screen. Screenshot it.
   Can you read it easily? Do the pictures of the buttons look like the ones on your phone?
3. **Do what it says**: tap the page button at the left of the address bar (on newer iPhones it looks like a jigsaw
   piece over two lines; tap the very top or bottom edge of the screen if you can't see the bar), then Share, then
   View More (the last round button), then "Add to Home Screen" at the bottom, then "Add". On older iPhones there is a
   Share button right in the bar instead. Tell us if any step was different on your phone.
4. **Open KRELWORLD from your Home Screen** (the new icon). Check:
   - it fills the screen with no Safari bars;
   - the box does NOT pop up again;
   - if you hold the phone upright, a "turn your phone sideways" screen shows, and the game carries on when you
     turn it back.
   - Note: the Home Screen app has its own saves, separate from Safari. That is normal on iPhone.
5. **Play for ten minutes**: start a new game. At home, put your left thumb anywhere on the left half and move it:
   you should walk. Walk up to the telly: a button saying WATCH (or similar) appears bottom right, and the little
   bubble over your head says "Tap: …". Tap either one. Go outside, walk around town.
   - Does the game ever **reload by itself** or show "A problem repeatedly occurred"? (That means the phone ran out
     of memory: the most important thing to tell us.)
   - Does it get **hot**? Does it stutter? (Look at `fps` in the box.)
6. **Sound**:
   - Is the music too loud even when you turn Music down in Settings?
   - Flip the silent switch on the side of the phone: do the sound effects go quiet while the music keeps playing?
   - Lock the phone for a minute, unlock: does the sound come back?
   - Do the characters' voices play?
7. **Edges**: tap things near the very bottom of the screen (just above the line at the bottom). Do the taps work?
   Turn the phone the other way round (notch on the other side): are the buttons still clear of the notch?
8. **Pause**: tap the two bars (top right). The pause menu should have "Add to Home Screen" in it.

## Android (about 10 minutes)

1. **Open the link in Chrome** with `?mobilediag` on the end. Hold the phone sideways. Tap the screen to enter: the game
   should go **full screen** and stay sideways.
2. **The "Add KRELWORLD to your Home Screen" box** should pop up over the title. Screenshot it. Does it show a yellow
   **INSTALL** button, or written steps? Either is fine: tell us which.
3. **Install it** (INSTALL, or the steps). Open it from the Home Screen: full screen, sideways? Any save you made in
   Chrome should still be there (CONTINUE on the title).
4. **Back gesture**: swipe in from the side (or press Back). The game should pause, not close.
5. **Vibration**: tap the action button bottom right when it appears. Do you feel a tiny buzz?
6. **Play for ten minutes** as on the iPhone (home, telly, town). Any reload by itself? Hot? Stuttering?
7. If you leave full screen (swipe down from the top), a small square button should appear top right to go back in.

## Extra, if you have time

- **From a chat app**: send yourself the link in Instagram, Messenger or WhatsApp and open it there. The box should say
  "Open KRELWORLD in Safari first" (or Chrome). Try the COPY LINK button. Screenshot it.
- **Bigger text preview**: open the link with `?mobileread=1` on the end (or `&mobileread=1`). Text should be bigger
  everywhere. Is it easier to read? Does anything overlap or get cut off? Screenshot the title and the settings menu.
  (This is a preview: it is not switched on for players yet.)
- **Chapter Select**: on the title, tap CHAPTER SELECT. When the keyboard comes up for the password, can you still see
  the box you are typing into?

## What to send back

1. For each phone: the model and system version.
2. The screenshots: the read-out box (start, home, town), the Add to Home Screen box, anything that looked wrong.
3. Yes / no answers:
   - Did the game ever reload by itself or crash? (When, and where in the game?)
   - Did the Add to Home Screen steps match your phone? Which step was different?
   - Home Screen app: full screen? Sideways? Did the box stay away the second time?
   - iPhone: music too loud even when turned down? Effects silent with the silent switch? Sound back after locking?
   - Taps near the bottom edge working?
   - Android: full screen on the first tap? INSTALL button or written steps? Back = pause? Buzz on tap?
   - Did walking feel natural? Anything you tried to tap that did nothing?
4. The lowest `fps` you saw, and roughly where.
